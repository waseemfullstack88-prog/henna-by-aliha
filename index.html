About The Artist

Passion, Precision & Artistry in Every Stroke

Dedicated to personalized mehndi artistry that transforms milestone moments into unforgettable memories.

Welcome to Henna by Aliha. Henna has always been more than an adornment to me—it is a cherished cultural tradition of joy, warmth, and celebration. Based in Muzaffargarh, Pakistan, I specialize in crafting bespoke mehndi art that complements each individual's unique story. Whether you are walking down the aisle as a bride or celebrating with loved ones, each pattern is drawn with dedication, natural organic henna, and meticulous precision.

🎨 Signature Artistry Style
A signature blend of royal bridal intricacy and airy Arabic flow, featuring fine botanical motifs, harmonious negative spacing, and deep natural stains.
👰 Dedicated Expertise
Experienced in bespoke bridal, delicate Arabic, festive party, and custom event mehndi artistry.
